## My Review

FFXVI took the series in a bold new direction with its real-time action combat and darker, more mature story. While it's different from traditional FF games, I really enjoyed my time with it.

## What I Love

- **Combat** - The Eikon battles are INSANE
- **Clive** - Best protagonist in years
- **Music** - Soken killed it once again
- **Visuals** - Absolutely stunning

## What Could Be Better

- More traditional RPG elements
- Side quests were a bit fetch-quest heavy
- Wanted more party member interaction

## Stream Highlights

The boss fights in this game were incredible to stream. Chat went wild during the Titan fight!

## Verdict

A fantastic game that shows FF can evolve while keeping its heart.
