## My Review

Stardew Valley is the game that got me into cozy gaming. Created by one person (ConcernedApe is a legend), this farming sim has everything: farming, fishing, mining, combat, romance, and so much heart.

## What I Love

- **The music** - The soundtrack is absolutely beautiful
- **The characters** - Each villager has depth and their own story
- **The freedom** - Play however you want, no pressure
- **Multiplayer** - Playing with friends is amazing!

## Stream Highlights

This is one of my most requested stream games. Chat loves helping me make farm decisions (and judging my crop layouts).

## Verdict

If you haven't played Stardew Valley, what are you waiting for? It's a masterpiece.
